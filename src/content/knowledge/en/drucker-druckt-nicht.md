## Look at the device

Check the printer itself for what is visible: paper, an open cover, a message on the display. That
explains most cases.

## Do not send many jobs in a row

Check whether your device still shows the job. Several identical jobs in a row make the situation
less clear, not better.

## Ask for help with context

Tell the person helping you:

- which device you used,
- which message was visible,
- roughly when you tried.

Do not send private documents along.
