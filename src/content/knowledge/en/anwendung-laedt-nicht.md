## Check another page first

Does another page load on the same device? If nothing loads, the problem is probably your
connection – not the application.

## Look at the status

Open **Status** in the workspace. Note the time of the last measurement. “Unknown” is a state of its
own and does not mean “down”.

## Choose the next step

- If a disruption is known, try again later.
- If the state is unclear or it only affects you, talk to the person who set up your access.

## What you do not need to do

You do not need to try addresses or change settings. Technical details are not part of your entry.
