VYRX shows the offers that match your access. Other people may see a different selection without
your workspace being incomplete. Which group opens which application is decided by the person who
manages access.

## Three things that are not the same

- **Not visible:** there is no release for your access. Another link will not change that.
- **Not reachable:** the offer is released but does not answer right now.
- **Unknown:** there is no current measurement. That does not automatically mean something is down.

The status view only helps for offers you have access to.

## Whom to ask

If you want to use an offer you cannot see, talk to the person who set up your access. They can
check the release.
