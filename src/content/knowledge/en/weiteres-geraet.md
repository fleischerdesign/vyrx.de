## Open the start page on the other device

Open **vyrx.de** on that device and choose the way into the workspace. Do not use a saved address
of a single application.

## Sign in with your own access

Follow the sign-in that appears. Do not share your access: which offers you reach depends on who is
signed in.

## Check your offers

After signing in you will find your released applications. If something is missing, first check that
you signed in with the right access.

## If signing in fails

If signing in already fails, talk to the person who set up your access. There is deliberately no
public self-registration.
