## Open “Applications”

Choose **Applications** in the workspace. It lists the offers that match your access.

## Search for what you want to do

Search for the task, or read the short descriptions. You do not need to know an application’s
technical name.

## Open the matching offer

Choose the offer that fits. If a second sign-in is required, follow the hints on the target page.

## If the offer is missing

Not everyone sees the same. A missing offer may simply not be released for your access. That is not
a fault – see “Why do I not see some offers?”.
