## Open media requests

Choose **Media requests** in the workspace. This offer is not released for everyone; if you do not
see it, your access has no release.

## Search and place your request

Search for the title inside the offer and place your request there. The exact controls live in the
application itself – they can change, so this article does not describe single buttons.

## When it becomes available

A request is not fulfilled immediately. When a title is ready depends on the source; VYRX
deliberately promises no processing time.
